Our Story

Restaurant ni Bebeng was founded by Bebeng Santos, a home cook known in her community for turning simple Filipino dishes into unforgettable meals. What began in a modest kitchen grew from countless family gatherings, where food was always the center of conversation, laughter, and connection.

Bebeng believed that cooking was not just about feeding people, but about caring for them. Her recipes—learned from her parents and grandparents—were never written down at first. They were taught by experience, taste, and patience. These same recipes became the foundation of the restaurant’s menu.

As demand grew, Bebeng was joined by her family. Her husband helped manage daily operations, while her children assisted in service, sourcing ingredients, and maintaining quality. The restaurant became a true family effort, built on trust, hard work, and shared values.

Over the years, Restaurant ni Bebeng welcomed dedicated staff who became part of the family. Cooks, servers, and managers were trained not only in food preparation, but in hospitality—treating every guest with warmth and respect. This culture allowed the restaurant to grow into multiple branches without losing its identity.

Today, Restaurant ni Bebeng stands as a celebration of Filipino tradition, community, and togetherness. Every dish served carries the hands and hearts of the people behind it—honoring the past while continuing to serve future generations.

Our Mission

At Restaurant ni Bebeng, our mission is to preserve and share authentic Filipino flavors through honest cooking and warm hospitality. We are committed to serving quality meals made from fresh ingredients, prepared with care, and rooted in tradition.

We aim to create a welcoming place where families, friends, and communities can gather, enjoy comforting food, and feel at home. By valuing people—our customers, staff, and partners—we strive to deliver consistent taste, genuine service, and a dining experience that reflects true Filipino culture.

Our Values

Quality

We use fresh ingredients and consistent cooking methods. Quality is controlled, not assumed.

Family & Community

We treat our customers and staff like family. Respect and care define how we work and serve.

Integrity

Hospitality

Consistency
